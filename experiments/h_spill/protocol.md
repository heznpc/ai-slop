# H-Spill: Cross-Format Contamination — Locked Protocol

**Pre-registered**: 2026-05-21
**Source of truth**: this file. Both Perspectives H3 and Framework H-Spill reference this protocol.

---

## Hypothesis (verbatim from papers/perspectives/paper/main.tex H3 and papers/framework/paper/main.tex H-Spill)

> H3 / H-Spill: AI slop propagates across formats through content transformation pipelines -- AI-generated academic paper becomes AI-generated news article becomes AI-generated blog post becomes AI-generated social carousel. Each transformation reduces the statistical signal available to format-specific detectors.

## Falsification rule

Reject H-Spill if, across 20 transformation chains, the per-chain Spearman correlation between **stage index** (0 = academic, 1 = news, 2 = blog, 3 = carousel) and **mean detection probability** (averaged across the 2-judge panel) yields $\rho \ge 0$ at $\alpha = 0.05$ under a one-sided test, OR if the stage-level mean detection probability does not exhibit a monotonically decreasing pattern (Mann-Kendall test).

## Data source — seeds (free)

- **arXiv API** (`http://export.arxiv.org/api/query`).
- 20 abstracts from CS.CL (Computation and Language) and CS.AI categories, restricted to publication years 2024-2026.
- Seed selection: most recent 20 papers matching the category filter, no further selection.
- Cached to `results/seeds.json`.
- Snapshot date recorded in `results/run_metadata.json`.

## Generation chain (Claude Sonnet 4.6, 60 calls total)

For each of the 20 seeds, three transformation prompts are issued sequentially to Claude Sonnet 4.6 with `--output-format json` for native usage capture. Temperature default. The output of stage $k$ is the input to stage $k+1$.

- **Stage 0**: original academic abstract (no LLM call)
- **Stage 1 → news article (~400 words)**:
  > "Rewrite the following academic abstract as a 400-word news article for a general audience. Preserve the technical claims but use accessible language. Do not add disclaimers or note that this is a rewrite. Reply with only the news article body, no headline, no metadata."
- **Stage 2 → personal blog post (~300 words)**:
  > "Rewrite the following news article as a 300-word personal blog post in first person. Keep the core finding but make it feel like a personal observation. Do not add disclaimers. Reply with only the blog post body."
- **Stage 3 → 5 carousel captions (~50 words each)**:
  > "Convert the following blog post into 5 social media carousel captions, 50 words each, suitable for Instagram or Naver card news. Number them 1 through 5. Do not add disclaimers."

Generation outputs cached per-seed under `results/chains/<seed_id>/stage_<N>.txt`. Native CLI envelope cached to `stage_<N>.meta.json`.

## Detection — 2-judge LLM-as-judge panel

For each of the 20 chains × 4 stages = 80 text artifacts, a 2-judge panel scores "probability this text is AI-generated":

- **Judge A**: Claude Opus 4.7 (`claude --print --output-format json --model claude-opus-4-7`)
- **Judge B**: Gemini 2.5 Pro (`gemini -m gemini-2.5-pro -o json -p`)

Identical prompt to both judges:

> "You are evaluating whether a piece of text was generated by an AI (LLM) or written by a human. Return only a JSON object with exactly two fields: `ai_probability` (float between 0.0 and 1.0; 1.0 = definitely AI, 0.0 = definitely human, 0.5 = uncertain) and `reasoning` (1-2 sentence justification). Reply with the JSON object only, no other text. TEXT TO EVALUATE: <text>"

160 judge calls total (80 cells × 2 judges). Per-call native envelope cached to `results/judgments/<seed_id>_<stage>_<judge>.meta.json` with `input_tokens, output_tokens, total_cost_usd, duration_ms`. Aggregate to `results/judgments_raw.csv`.

**Panel mean**: per-cell `ai_probability_panel = mean(A_score, B_score)`.

## Stylometric statistics (free, local)

For each of the 80 text artifacts, compute (no external API):

- **mean_sentence_length**: words per sentence, mean
- **sentence_length_variance**: words per sentence, variance ("burstiness" proxy)
- **type_token_ratio**: distinct words / total words (lexical diversity)
- **word_count**: total words
- **char_count**: total characters

Stored in `results/stylo.csv`.

## Primary statistic

Per chain $i$ (i = 1..20), compute Spearman's $\rho_i$ between stage index $\{0,1,2,3\}$ and the per-stage panel-mean AI probability. The aggregate test statistic is the mean $\bar{\rho}$ across the 20 chains. Falsify if $\bar{\rho} \ge 0$ at one-sided $\alpha = 0.05$ using a permutation test (10{,}000 permutations of stage labels within each chain).

## Secondary statistics

- **Mann-Kendall trend test** on per-stage mean AI probability across the 4 stages (n=4 means).
- **Bootstrap 95% CI** for per-stage mean AI probability (10{,}000 resamples over the 20 chains, fixed seed 20260521).
- **Effect size**: mean Δ AI probability between stage 0 and stage 3.
- **Stylometric trends**: same Spearman test on each stylometric metric across stages, to see whether they degrade as predicted.

## Sample size and power

20 chains × 4 stages = 80 cells; for the per-chain Spearman with n=4 stages, only |ρ| = 1.0 is significant at α = 0.05 (one-tailed n=4 critical value). The aggregate test pools per-chain ρ and uses permutation; power against a true monotone decrease of 0.2 probability per stage is moderate but adequate for an exploratory test. A null result here would be interpretable but a positive result would be a non-trivial finding.

## Cost / wall-time budget

- Generation: 60 Sonnet 4.6 calls × ~$0.04 ≈ **$2.40**
- Judging: 80 Opus 4.7 calls × ~$0.27 ≈ **$21.60**; 80 Gemini 2.5 Pro calls free
- Total Anthropic billing budget: **~$25** (well under the user's "$10-30" guidance for the previous experiment)
- Wall time: 60 gen × 7s + 80 Opus × 7s + 80 Gemini × 25s ≈ **~50 minutes** serial

Observability is first-class: per-call `.meta.json` files capture native usage. Per-iteration progress is surfaced to the chat at least every 10 iterations (not just at end-of-run).

## Reproducibility

- `seeds.json`: arXiv paper IDs + abstract texts pinned at snapshot date
- `chains/<seed_id>/stage_<N>.txt`: generation outputs cached
- `judgments/<seed_id>_<stage>_<judge>.json`: parsed judge scores cached
- `*.meta.json`: native CLI usage envelopes per call
- `run_metadata.json`: timestamps, CLI versions, model identifiers, total cost
- `run.log`: per-event log including per-call wall time + cost line

## Failure modes

1. **Judge parse failure** (no valid JSON): 1 retry with stricter prompt; if still failing, mark cell as missing, document in `limitations.md`.
2. **API rate limit**: 3 retries with exponential backoff; if still failing on a cell, halt and report.
3. **Stylometric NaN** (zero-length text): mark as missing, exclude from per-format stats.
4. **arXiv API unreachable**: halt, do not silently substitute.

## What this protocol does NOT do

- Does not use paid detection APIs (GPTZero, Originality.AI). Detection signal comes from LLM-as-judge + stylometric statistics only.
- Does not include human raters.
- Does not test for prompt-variant robustness (single prompt per stage).
- Does not vary the generator model (Sonnet 4.6 only for generation).
- Does not measure cross-format transformations in other directions (e.g., carousel → blog).
