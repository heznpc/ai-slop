# H-Text: Adversarial Text Detection — Locked Protocol

**Pre-registered**: 2026-05-21
**Source of truth**: this file.

---

## Hypothesis (Framework Research Agenda, row 2)

> H-Text: Text detection falls below useful thresholds under adversarial conditions while video detection remains above 0.70.

## Scope reduction for Claude Code

This protocol tests the **text half** of H-Text. The video comparison half is already substantiated in the literature (FaceForensics++, DFDC, ASVspoof benchmarks all cited in Framework \S2.3; deepfake video AUROC 91--97% in-domain). Reproducing video AUROC under adversarial paraphrase would require a deepfake corpus + adversarial attack budget that is out of scope for an LLM-as-judge Claude Code experiment. The video side is therefore taken as a literature value (\textgreater 0.70 AUROC under standard adversarial conditions) and not re-tested here.

The operationalized test is:

> **H-Text-adv**: When AI-generated text is adversarially paraphrased, LLM-as-judge detection probability drops below the 0.70 useful threshold, falling below the literature-stated video detection floor.

## Falsification rule

Reject H-Text-adv if either of the following holds:

1. **Threshold test**: The mean panel ai-probability across all (chain, adversarial-variant) cells is $\geq 0.70$.
2. **Paired test**: A one-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank test (n=15 chains; baseline minus mean-of-variants) yields $p \geq 0.05$.

Both conditions must show effect (a) directionally below 0.70 AND (b) statistically below baseline for the hypothesis to be supported.

## Data — baseline reuse from H-Spill

Baseline AI-text comes from `experiments/h_spill/results/chains/<seed_id>/stage_1.txt` — these are the news-article rewrites generated by Claude Sonnet 4.6 from the 20 OpenAlex academic seeds. The 2-judge panel scores for these baselines are already cached at `experiments/h_spill/results/judgments/<seed_id>_1_<judge>.json`. **15 of the 20 H-Spill chains** are reused (first 15 by OpenAlex Work ID lexicographic order). No new generation cost for baselines.

## Adversarial variants — Claude Sonnet 4.6

For each of the 15 baseline texts, two adversarial variants are generated. Same Sonnet 4.6 invocation as H-Spill generation, temperature default, `--output-format json`:

- **Variant A (paraphrase)**:
  > "Rewrite the following text. Preserve every factual claim and the overall logical structure, but change the wording, sentence rhythm, and clause-level structure. Aim for a noticeable but content-equivalent stylistic shift. Do not add disclaimers or note that this is a rewrite. Reply with only the rewritten text body."

- **Variant B (colloquial / human-stylized)**:
  > "Rewrite the following text in a casual, conversational register, as if a slightly opinionated human writer were summarizing it for a friend. Use contractions, hedges (\"I think\", \"sort of\"), one or two intentional digressions, and occasional em-dashes. Preserve the factual claims but make the prose feel humanly idiosyncratic. Do not add disclaimers. Reply with only the rewritten text body."

30 Sonnet calls total (15 chains × 2 variants).

## Detection — 2-judge LLM-as-judge panel

For each of the 30 adversarial texts, a 2-judge panel scores AI probability using the **identical prompt template as H-Spill** (`experiments/h_spill/protocol.md`, "Detection" section). Judges:

- **Judge A**: Claude Opus 4.7 (`claude --print --no-session-persistence --output-format json --model claude-opus-4-7`)
- **Judge B**: Gemini 2.5 Flash (`gemini -m gemini-2.5-flash -o json -p`)

60 judge calls total (30 cells × 2 judges). Per-call native envelope cached to `results/judgments/<seed_id>_<variant>_<judge>.meta.json`.

**Panel mean**: per (chain, variant) cell, `panel_ai_p = mean(judge_A, judge_B)`.

## Primary analysis

Per chain $i$ (15 chains), compute:

- `baseline_panel_ai_p_i`: panel mean over the 2 H-Spill cached judges of stage 1 (news article)
- `variant_A_panel_ai_p_i`: panel mean for adversarial variant A
- `variant_B_panel_ai_p_i`: panel mean for adversarial variant B
- `mean_variant_ai_p_i`: mean of the two variant panel means

Tests:

- **Threshold**: Across all 15 chains, is $\text{mean}(\text{mean\_variant\_ai\_p}) < 0.70$? Bootstrap 95\% CI via 10{,}000 resamples (seed `20260521`).
- **Paired Wilcoxon**: One-sided test (alternative: baseline $>$ variant mean). $\alpha = 0.05$.
- **Per-variant breakdown**: Same threshold + paired tests separately for variant A and variant B (descriptive, not used for the falsification rule).

## Secondary statistics

- Sentence-length variance ("burstiness"), TTR, mean sentence length per (chain, condition) cell, to check whether adversarial variants do in fact shift stylometric features.
- Per-rater agreement (Pearson r) baseline vs each variant, to detect rater-specific bias.

## Sample size and power

15 chains × 2 variants = 30 (chain, variant) cells. For the paired Wilcoxon with n=15, a moderate effect size (Cohen's d $\approx 0.7$) is detectable at $\alpha = 0.05$ with power $\approx 0.78$. The threshold test does not require a power calculation -- the directional claim is descriptive: either mean variant ai\_p falls below 0.70 or it does not.

## Cost / wall-time budget

- Generation: 30 Sonnet 4.6 calls × ~$0.04 ≈ **$1.20**
- Judging: 60 calls × 2 judges = 120 ... wait, recount. 60 cells × 2 judges = 120 — no. 30 adversarial texts × 2 judges = 60 judge calls
  - Opus 4.7 × 30 × ~$0.27 ≈ **$8.10**
  - Gemini 2.5 Flash × 30 free
- **Total Anthropic billing ≈ $9.30** (well within the previous H-Spill envelope)
- Wall time: 30 gen × ~10s + 60 judge × ~10s = 15 min serial; 3-5 min with 4-wide concurrency

## Failure modes

1. **Parse failure**: 1 retry with stricter instruction; if still failing, log to limitations and skip.
2. **Rate limit / quota exhaustion** (lessons from H-Spill): if Gemini Flash quota exhausts, halt and document. No further substitution chain.
3. **Adversarial variant indistinguishable from baseline** (humanly assessed): document but do not silently re-prompt. The rubric for "adversarial" is fixed by the locked Variant A / Variant B prompts above.

## What this protocol does NOT do

- Does not test video adversarial detection (literature value used as comparator).
- Does not vary the generator (Sonnet 4.6 only).
- Does not include human raters.
- Does not test prompt sensitivity of the adversarial transformation prompts (single locked rubric).
- Does not test on raw AI-generated text. Baselines are H-Spill stage 1 (rewrites of academic abstracts), so they may already carry some "academic-flavored AI" structure. Generalization to other AI-text genres is not claimed.
